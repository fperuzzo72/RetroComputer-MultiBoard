lib/pc8086 is M5PaperDOS's 8086 emulator core, whose CPU and BIOS follow
8086tiny, and whose BIOS image is 8086tiny's.

M5PaperDOS - https://github.com/omeriko9/M5PaperDOS (vendored through the
fork https://github.com/fperuzzo72/M5Paper_8086). Its README states
"License: MIT"; the repository carries no separate license file.
